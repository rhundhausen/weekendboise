About

Things to do this weekend, and every day.

What is WeekendBoise?

WeekendBoise lists upcoming events in Boise, Meridian, Nampa, Caldwell, Eagle, Garden City, Kuna, Star, Emmett, and the towns around them, out to Mountain Home, Payette, Weiser, and even Valley County. Concerts, theater, games, markets, festivals, library programs, classes, races, auctions and estate sales: if a public calendar lists it, it shows up here.

Every night at midnight Boise time, the site reads {{ $roundedSources }} public calendars, feeds, and APIs (see the sources), merges events that more than one source lists, and drops events that are over. Every listing links to the organizer's own page, which has the full details and the final word on times, tickets, and changes.

The site stores only what the list needs: title, times, venue, city, category, and a short description. It keeps no ticket prices, photos, or contact details, and it never works around a site that asks not to be read.

How it started

WeekendBoise began as a personal project by Richard Hundhausen, with a simple goal: to know what was happening around Boise without hunting through dozens of separate websites. One calendar led to another, then a few dozen more, and a small side project grew into what you see today: hundreds of sources and thousands of events, refreshed every night.

Size

An estimate of the crowd, not a count or a capacity. It is a guess, worked out from the first of these that applies:

  1. A small-format event (a workshop, cooking class, tour, meetup, board game night, or library program) is Small, even at a big venue.
  2. The venue's usual crowd, for the venues we know, from approximate capacities.
  3. The kind of event: a rodeo or a race is usually Large, an air show Extra Large.
  4. The category's usual size.

Cost

The cheapest adult ticket at face value, before fees, sorted into a tier. Only the tier is kept, never the price. It comes from the source's own price data, or from a price written in the event's description. Kids, senior, student, and member prices are ignored; for a range, the low end counts. A suggested donation, or free entry with paid parking or food, is Free.

Category

Each event has one category, from its source (a theater's events are Performing Arts) or, for sources that mix many kinds, from words in its title and the source's own labels. A holiday concert is Holiday and Seasonal first.

City

Where the event is, from its address or venue. Valley County groups McCall, Cascade, Donnelly, Tamarack, and the other mountain towns as one place. Other means the source did not say and the event's page did not settle it. The City list shows only cities with upcoming events, and its counts follow the other filters you pick.

Other things you may see

  • Include Meetups: Meetup group events are hidden until this is checked, because many groups are only partly public.
  • A title with no link: the event's page stopped loading, and no other source had a working one.
  • Time: Boise time. An all-day event shows no time; a multi-day event runs from its first day to its last.

Meetup events

Events from Meetup groups are listed because the group posts them publicly, but some groups let only members RSVP or see the exact location. You may need a free Meetup account, and to join the group, before you can go.

Suggestions

Spot a wrong listing, miss an event or a calendar, or have an idea? Create a GitHub issue (it needs a free GitHub account). Run a site listed here and want it left out? Say so in an issue and it is removed.
